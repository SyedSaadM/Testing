Why Lumen Minds

We publish for the classroom first.

Every title is planned, written and checked with a learner and a teacher in mind, long before it gets a cover.

  • Written with classroom teachers

    Authors and reviewers know the syllabus and the questions learners actually ask.

  • Plain language, careful layout

    Explanations that learners of every reading level can follow, with diagrams placed where you need them.

  • Built around the syllabus

    Units follow the course, so planning a term is quicker and learners know what to expect.

  • Support beyond the book

    Teacher support and extra practice come with each series.
